Featured Adventures

Beginner | Half Day

Family River Discovery

Learn the basics on friendly water with time for swimming, wildlife watching, and a riverside snack. This trip is designed for families and first-time paddlers who want a relaxed start.

Intermediate | Full Day

Los Dientes del Diablo

Paddle a varied canyon route with technical turns, exciting waves, and a quiet lunch stop. Guides coach every rapid so the group can improve its timing and confidence throughout the day.

Advanced | Full Day

Big Mallard Challenge

Take on continuous rapids, strong currents, and precise team maneuvers with our most experienced guides. Previous rafting experience and a confident swimming ability are required.
